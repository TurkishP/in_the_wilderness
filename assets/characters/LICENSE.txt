Files from Universal Base Characters
  male.gltf/.bin, female.gltf/.bin         the "Superhero" male and female bodies
  hair_*.gltf/.bin, beard.*, brows_*.*     hairstyles, beard and eyebrows ("Origin at 0" set)
  tex/*.png                                skin, eye and hair textures

  Changes, all made by tools/prepare_characters.py:
    - files renamed
    - texture references removed from the glTF files (the game assigns materials in code)
    - textures resized from 2048 px to 1024 px
    - skin base color lightened, so one texture can be tinted to every skin tone
    - eye_0 to eye_4: the brown eye texture recolored into five eye colors
    - the roughness textures and the FBX/Unity/Unreal files are not included

File from Universal Animation Library
  animations.glb                           unmodified (the Godot build of the Standard version)

CC0 does not require credit. It is given here so the origin of the files stays clear.
The clothes worn by the characters are not part of these kits; they are generated by
scripts/human.gd.
